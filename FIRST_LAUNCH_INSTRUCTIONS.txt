Safe Drive Ejector Tool — First Launch Guide (Free GitHub Release)
=============================================================================

Safe Drive Ejector Tool is a 100% free, community-driven open-source project.
Because it is distributed directly via GitHub Releases without a paid $99/year
Apple Developer subscription or expensive commercial Windows EV certificate,
your operating system's built-in security features (macOS Gatekeeper / Windows
SmartScreen) will display a standard initial verification alert on first run.

This is completely normal for community open-source software. You DO NOT need
to disable System Integrity Protection (SIP), turn off Gatekeeper globally, or
compromise your operating system's security in any way.

=============================================================================
🍎 macOS (Apple Silicon arm64 & Intel x86_64)
=============================================================================

On first launch, macOS Gatekeeper may display:
    "Safe Drive Ejector" Not Opened
    Apple could not verify "Safe Drive Ejector" is free of malware that may
    harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

TO OPEN THE APPLICATION (Choose either Method 1 or Method 2):

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method 1: Standard macOS Security Settings (GUI — Recommended)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Drag "Safe Drive Ejector.app" into your "Applications" folder.
2. Double-click "Safe Drive Ejector.app".
3. When the alert appears, click "Done" (or "Cancel").
4. Open System Settings ( Apple Menu > System Settings).
5. Go to "Privacy & Security" and scroll down to the "Security" section.
6. Look for the message:
   '"Safe Drive Ejector" was blocked to protect your Mac.'
7. Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
8. Enter your Mac login password or use Touch ID, then click "Open".

* You only need to perform this approval ONCE.
* Subsequent launches will start automatically from your Menu Bar and at login.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method 2: One-Line Terminal Command (Fastest for Power Users)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you prefer using Terminal, simply clear the macOS browser quarantine flag:

    xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Safe Drive Ejector.app"

Then open the application normally from Finder, Launchpad, or Spotlight.

=============================================================================
🪟 Windows (x64)
=============================================================================

When you run "install.bat" or launch "SafeDriveEjector.exe", Microsoft Defender
SmartScreen may display:
    "Windows protected your PC"
    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting.

TO RUN:
1. Click the "More info" link under the warning message.
2. Click the "Run anyway" button that appears.

To configure automatic startup and sleep/wake event listeners:
- Double-click "install.bat" (no administrator privileges required).
